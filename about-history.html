Our story

From one stool on Street 310 to five branches

Fourteen years, three kitchens, one recipe for broth that has not changed — only the number of people around the table.

How it started

A family stall with one burner

In 2012 Auntie Nha opened a six-table stall in Boeung Keng Kang with two gas burners, a borrowed freezer and a broth recipe she had cooked for family dinners her whole life. The name was a joke between cousins — “9999” because there would always be “nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine more reasons to come back”.

The stall served 40 guests on its first weekend. By the end of that year, neighbours were queuing on the pavement with plastic chairs, and we hired the first four full-time staff.

“We never advertised. People brought their friends, the friends brought their families, and the tables filled themselves.”
Auntie Nha, founder
The first recipe, 2012
Timeline

Fourteen years, step by step

Every stage below is still written on the wall of the BKK1 branch — the original wall, with the original paint.

  1. 2012

    The first six tables

    Auntie Nha opens a family hotpot stall on Street 310, Boeung Keng Kang, with one chicken broth, one mala broth and 40 guests in the first weekend.

  2. 2014

    All-you-can-eat is born

    We stop selling by the plate and switch to one price with unlimited refills. Weekend waiting time reaches 45 minutes and the vinyl stools are replaced by real chairs.

  3. 2016

    Two more branches in Phnom Penh

    Toul Kork and Toul Svay Prey open within eight months of each other. The sauce bar grows from four jars to fourteen, and handmade noodles move in-house.

  4. 2018

    Noodles by hand, beef by knife

    We build a small noodle room behind the flagship kitchen and hire our first dedicated butchers. Beef starts being sliced to order instead of pre-portioned.

  5. 2020

    The year of delivery

    When dine-in closed, we packed broth in sealed pots and delivered 1,200 hotpot sets in four months. Every single staff member kept their job.

  6. 2022

    Franchising begins

    The first franchised branch opens in Sen Sok inside Aeon Mall 2. We write the operations manual — the same one our franchise partners use today.

  7. 2024

    Across the country

    Siem Reap becomes branch number five, our first outside Phnom Penh, with a rooftop terrace where the grill runs until midnight.

  8. 2026

    Today: 5 branches, 1,250 guests a week

    Six broths, 48 ingredients on the line, and a booking system that takes under a minute. The family stall is now 180 staff — and Auntie Nha still checks the broth.
