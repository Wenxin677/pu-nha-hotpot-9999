About the restaurant

One pot, everybody in

Hotpot is the most sociable meal there is: everybody reaches into the same pot, nobody eats alone, and the pot keeps going until the last person is full.

We started as a family stall with plastic stools, one burner and broth that my grandmother stirred for six hours. Today each of our five branches serves over 250 guests a day with the same promise: keep it fresh, keep it generous and keep the table laughing.

  • All-you-can-eat: order as much as you like for 90 minutes.
  • Fresh every morning: vegetables and fish bought daily at the market.
  • Six broths: from mild chicken to full-on mala — mix two in one pot.
  • Everyone is welcome: vegan, halal-friendly and kids' options on every menu.

How it works

From your phone to a full table in three steps

  1. Book online

    Fill in the booking form below and choose your branch. You will get a confirmation call or Telegram message within 15 minutes during opening hours.

  2. Pick your broths

    Your table is set with a divided pot. Choose two broths, then hit the buffet line: meat, seafood, vegetables, noodles and the sauce bar are all unlimited.

  3. Keep the pot going

    90 minutes, unlimited refills. When you are ready, finish with noodles in the last of the broth and something sweet from the dessert counter.
