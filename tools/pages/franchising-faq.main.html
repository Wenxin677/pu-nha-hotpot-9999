Part 1 — Getting started

Money, space and time

How much does a Pu Nha Hotpot 9999 franchise cost?

There is a one-off franchise fee plus the fit-out and equipment for your branch. The fee starts at $12,000 for a kiosk format and $25,000 for a standard dine-in branch; a flagship licence with a grill counter is $38,000. Total opening investment including fit-out, equipment and the first stock order runs from roughly $48,000 to $165,000.

See the requirements page for the full breakdown of every figure.

How much space do I need?

A kiosk works from 45 m², a standard branch needs 180 m² or more for 80–120 seats, and a flagship with a rooftop or grill counter needs 320 m² or more. The kitchen must be at least 25% of the total floor area and must have a floor drain and a 3-phase supply.

What is the royalty and marketing fee?

A flat 5% of net sales as royalty and 2% for the national marketing fund, reported and paid monthly. There is no fee on delivery orders in your first year — we want partners to build that channel without extra cost.

How long does it take to open?

Between 6 and 9 months: 2–4 weeks for the site visit and approval, 2 weeks for the contract, then 3–5 months to build and fit out. Training starts 6 weeks before opening day.

Part 2 — Support & training

What you get from us

What support do I actually receive?

Site selection and layout drawings, our full operations manual, 4 weeks of training for up to 6 people, opening-week support from a launch team (7 days on site), supply of broth bases, sauces and signature dumplings, menu and pricing updates, and a monthly performance review with your area manager.

How does the training work?

Four weeks at our BKK1 training kitchen: week 1 broth, sauce bar and hygiene; week 2 kitchen stations and butchery; week 3 front of house, booking system and service; week 4 as a full team running a simulated service. Every graduate receives a certificate and a copy of the manual.

Who orders the food?

You order directly from the approved supplier list through our purchasing system. Broth bases, sauces and dumplings come from our central kitchen in Phnom Penh; fresh meat, seafood and vegetables come from approved local suppliers so they stay fresh and support local farmers.

How much marketing do I have to pay for myself?

The 2% marketing fund covers national campaigns and the shared social-media calendar. Local store-level marketing — community events, local flyers, your grand opening — is planned with your area manager and paid by the branch. We provide editable artwork for all of it.

Can I open more than one branch?

Yes. Partners who pass the 12-month review can apply for a second territory at a 50%-reduced franchise fee. Territory exclusivity is available at district level for multi-branch partners.

Part 3 — Contracts & next steps

Rules, length and what happens next

How long is the contract, and can it be renewed?

The standard contract is 10 years with a 5-year renewal option at no extra franchise fee, provided the branch passes its annual review. Kiosk contracts run for 5 years with the same renewal terms.

What happens if a branch is underperforming?

You get early warning, not silence: we review every branch monthly and a branch below target for two consecutive quarters receives a support plan — menu review, pricing, local marketing and 30 days of on-site coaching from an area manager. The goal is always to fix the branch, not to end the contract.

Can I change the menu or the prices?

You must keep the core menu — the six broths, the sauce bar and the signature dumplings — so guests get the same experience everywhere. Prices are agreed by region each year, and up to three local dishes can be added with our approval.

What do I need to prepare before applying?

A company registration or business licence, a proposed location or city, proof of funds for the format you want, and one partner who can work full-time on the branch. We ask for a short business profile — two pages is enough.

How do I start the conversation?

Email franchise@punhahotpot9999.com or call +855 12 999 901 and ask for the franchising team. We reply within one working day, then book a 45-minute call and a site visit at an existing branch.
