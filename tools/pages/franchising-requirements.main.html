Investment packages

Choose the format that matches your location and budget. The franchise fee is paid in two parts: 60% on signing, 40% on the day your site is handed over for fit-out. Fit-out, equipment and stock are paid directly by you to approved contractors and suppliers — we never take a margin on them.

Step by step

The application process

  1. Send your profile. Email your city, proposed site and budget to franchise@punhahotpot9999.com.
  2. Introduction call (45 min). We walk through the numbers, the format and the timeline — and answer everything the FAQ did not cover.
  3. Site visit. You visit an existing branch, and our team visits your proposed site and returns a layout sketch.
  4. Financial review. We check your funding plan and agree the format and the territory.
  5. Contract & first payment. Signature and the 60% franchise fee, then the site is handed over to your contractor.
  6. Build & train (3–5 months). Fit-out runs in parallel with four weeks of training at the academy.
  7. Opening week. Our launch team is on site for seven days, then your area manager visits monthly.
