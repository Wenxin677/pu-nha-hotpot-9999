Getting there

Practical notes before you come

  • Tuk-tuk & moto: all branches have a drop-off bay; BKK1 has a covered waiting area for the rain.
  • Buses: Phnom Penh city bus line 3 stops 200 m from BKK1 and line 5 runs past Toul Kork.
  • Siem Reap: the branch is a 10-minute walk from Pub Street — rooftop grill opens at 17:00, no booking needed for the terrace.
  • Wheelchair access: BKK1, Sen Sok and Siem Reap are step-free with accessible toilets.
  • Bringing children: high chairs and a soft-cooked children's pot are available at every branch, free of charge.
