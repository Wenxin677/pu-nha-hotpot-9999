Lost something at a table?

Items found in a branch are kept for 30 days at that branch. Call the branch directly — the phone numbers are on the locations page — or email care@punhahotpot9999.com with the date and branch and we will check the log for you.

Working with us

We hire in the kitchen, on the floor and in delivery. Send a short introduction and your availability to jobs@punhahotpot9999.com, or ask at any branch — most of our managers started as servers.
